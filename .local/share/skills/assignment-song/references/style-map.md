# Theme to style map

Starting points, not rules. Pick the genre whose attitude mirrors the conflict in the assignment, then check the language rule in `SKILL.md` (German needs a genre that carries German without sounding like Schlager). Tempos are targets for Suno; it drifts by up to 10 %.

| Theme of the assignment (examples from DIGGB and Informatik) | Style | BPM | Why it fits | German? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Social media business, ads, attention, AGB, consumer rights (`05_social_business`, `28_agb_analyse`, `15_tiktok_analyse`) | German skate punk / melodic hardcore, gang shouts | 180–190 | Anti-corporate attitude, "you are the product" anger | yes (reference example) |
| Filter bubbles, echo chambers, fake news, deepfakes (`06_filterblasen`, `16_fake_news1`, `11_deepfake_check`) | Post-hardcore or dance-punk with distorted bass | 130–175 | Tension between inside and outside the bubble, shouted breakouts | yes (post-hardcore), dance-punk better in English |
| AI, hallucinations, fact checks (`09_ki_deepdive`, `10_ki_faktencheck`, `17_faktencheck`) | Nu-disco / electro-funk with vocoder | 124–128 | Smooth surface hiding errors; satire | English ("Fact or Fake" worked); German only with anti-Schlager measures |
| Passwords, phishing, cyber security, encryption, public key (`27_phishing_detektiv`, `09_caesar_code`, `10_public_key`, Informatik Cybersicherheit) | Industrial metal / Neue Deutsche Härte or cyberpunk darksynth | 125–140 (NDH), 110–130 (darksynth) | Threat, armour, hacker aesthetics | yes (NDH carries German natively) |
| Privacy, DSGVO, digital footprint, profiles (`12_dsgvo_basics`, `18_digitaler_fussabdruck`, `30_profil_privatsphaere`, Informatik Datenschutz) | Hardcore punk or Deutschrap over drum and bass | 170–180 | Defiance, "my data, my rules" | yes |
| Networks, packets, DNS, cloud, WLAN/VPN (`02_netzwerk_pakete`, `03_dns_abfrage`, `27_netzwerk_basics`, `27_wlan_vpn_schutz`) | Drum and bass / breakbeat with rapid vocals | 172–176 | Speed of packets, routing as a chase | yes (rap or chant vocals), sung lines get buried |
| Programming, algorithms, Scratch, Python, flowcharts, micro:bit (`09_scratch_start` to `13_scratch_variablen`, `12_python_start`, `20_algorithmus_basics`, `22_microbit_start`) | SID chiptune metal / video game metal | 160–180 | Retro computing, loops and logic as riffs | yes for metal vocals; English hook also fine |
| Binary, ASCII, codes (`14_binaer_system`, `15_ascii_code`, `16_geheim_code`) | Electro / techno with spoken robotic counting, or chiptune punk | 130–150 | Machine rhythm, counting in 0 and 1 | yes (spoken or shouted numbers) |
| Excel, charts, statistics, databases (`05_excel_statistik` to `07_excel_kreis`, `02_excel_absolut`, `25_datenbank_basics`) | Big beat / electronic rock with stomping drums | 130–140 | Precise grid, numbers that build columns | German chant vocals yes, avoid sung German dance-pop |
| Word, PowerPoint, layout, video editing, photography (`02_word_formatvorlagen`, `08_ppt_animation`, `23_video_schnitt1`, `07_foto_perspektiven`) | Indie rock / dance-punk with handclaps | 140–160 | Creative energy, cut and frame like a riff | yes (indie rock), dance-punk English |
| Copyright, Creative Commons, image rights (`18_creative_commons`, `29_urheberrecht_alltag`, `13_bildrechte_personen`) | Pop-punk with a courtroom call-and-response | 170–180 | Fairness for creators, clear rules as chant | yes |
| Hardware, software, ergonomics, sustainability, CO2 (`20_hardware_kunde`, `22_ergonomie_pass`, `17_digital_co2`) | Stoner/alt-metal groove or solarpunk folk-metal | 130–150 | Physical, heavy, earthy | yes |
| Folder structure, Windows basics for first-years (`02_dateien_ordnung`) | Fast pop-punk with a simple chant hook | 170–180 | Easy to shout along for 10-year-olds | yes (short words) |

Do not pick a genre only for the theme if it kills intelligibility: the key terms of the core task must stay understandable.
