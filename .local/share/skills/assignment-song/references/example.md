# Reference example: "Du bist das Produkt"

Peter (2026-11-09): "an example of how such songs could sound". Source note: `~/Obsidian/Adrixan/AI/Claude.ai/Projects/Music/Artifacts/Du bist das Produkt.md` (DIGGB 4, `05_social_business`).

Why it works:
- Genre with an attitude that mirrors the theme (punk against the platform business), German carried naturally, no Schlager signals.
- Hook = the core sentence of the worksheet.
- Pre-chorus answers question 1 in two shouted words; verse 2 answers question 2; the bridge lists every data type from the material as a rapid-fire chant; the breakdown explains the Vertiefung with one concrete invented profile; build-up and final chorus turn knowledge into control (why it matters).
- 185 BPM, instant start, breakdown keeps the tempo, hard stop.
